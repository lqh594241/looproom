02 / FIND YOUR PACE

Slow down without dropping the key.

Keep-pitch mode uses your browser’s audio pitch preservation. Pitch-following mode links pitch to speed: half speed is one octave lower.

Export your original-speed selection to a PCM WAV file. The metronome runs separately and is not mixed into your audio.

BEFORE YOU PRESS PLAY

A few things
to tune into.

Everything you need to know about this little studio.

Does slowing down change the pitch?

In keep-pitch mode, supported browsers preserve the track’s pitch while changing playback speed. The pitch-following mode disables that feature, so speed and pitch change together.

Can I change the key independently?

This version does not offer independent pitch shifting. In pitch-following mode the displayed semitone shift is 12 × log₂(playback rate), so changing pitch also changes tempo.

Is my audio uploaded?

No. Your file is opened as a local browser object URL and decoded on your device for the waveform and WAV export. There is no remote audio service.

Why is my loop not perfectly seamless?

The player uses browser media seeking, which is approximate. Tiny delays or clicks may occur at loop boundaries. This is a practice aid rather than a sample-accurate audio editor.

What is included in the WAV download?

Only the original audio between A and B, at its original speed and pitch, for up to 120 seconds. The export uses mono or stereo PCM; playback effects and metronome audio are not rendered.
